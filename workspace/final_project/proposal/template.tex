\section{Project Overview}
Indicate which example project your project is based on. Then, cover the following:
\begin{itemize}
    \item What is the problem and why is it important (motivate your project)?

    \item What is/are the key question(s) your project is trying to answer?

    \item Hypothesize what results you expect to see and explain why you think this will occur?

\section{Evaluation}
Good topics to cover:
\begin{itemize}
    \item What will you compare with (baseline)? Please explain the following.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item What will you hold constant for a fair comparison? For example, comparison of two accelerators should hold area constant or include area in the reported metric.
    \end{itemize}
    \item Plan three experiments to evaluate your idea. For each experiment, please make sure to:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Connect to class concepts that will help you analyze the results (\emph{e.g.}, data reuse explains how on-chip buffers reduces energy consumption).
        \item What metrics does the experiment affect (\emph{e.g.}, energy, latency, area)?
        \item How would you communicate the results? For example, describe a plot or table (including what the axes are).
        \item What trend do you expect to see, and why do you expect to see them?
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
